# 🌟 Overview

**Smart Issue City** is an intelligent civic engagement platform that empowers citizens to report public infrastructure issues quickly while enabling authorities to monitor, prioritize, and resolve them efficiently.

Instead of relying on traditional complaint systems, citizens can report issues with precise locations, categorize them, monitor progress in real time, and contribute to creating cleaner, safer, and smarter cities.

> **"Every report is a step toward a better city."**

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Urban areas face numerous public issues every day:

🚧 Road damages

💡 Broken street lights

🚮 Garbage accumulation

💧 Water leakage

🌳 Fallen trees

🚦 Traffic signal failures

🛣 Potholes

📍 Unreported civic issues

Traditional complaint mechanisms are often slow, non-transparent, and difficult to track.

**Smart Issue City solves this through digital reporting, location intelligence, and real-time tracking.**
